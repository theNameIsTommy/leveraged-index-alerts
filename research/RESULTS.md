# SMA200 band research

Generated 2026-10-06 by `research/backtest.py`. Re-run to refresh.

## Model

- BULL (close ≥ SMA200 + band) holds a 2x daily-reset product; BEAR/NEUTRAL holds cash.
- Trade 1 day after the signal close (alerts arrive overnight).
- 2x cost: borrowed leg pays the 13-week T-bill (^IRX) + 0.5%/yr, plus 0.6%/yr product fee.
- 0.15% of capital per switch. Cash earns the T-bill rate. No taxes.
- ATR-style band = k × 20-day mean absolute daily move (close-only, because MSCI history lacks highs/lows).
- Whipsaw = a switch reversed within 20 trading days. Worst streak = consecutive losing round trips.
- All CAGR figures are after costs. 'Cost drag' = CAGR lost to switch costs alone.
- In-sample history only. Past regimes, rates and volatility will differ.
